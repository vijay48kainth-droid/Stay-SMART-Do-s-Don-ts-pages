Think SMART before you tap, type or click.

Do

There are many things you should do online. This page will inform you of all the things that you should do online.

Some online actions can seem harmless at the time. But these can attract cyber criminals, which can lead to the loss of your personal information, scams, account theft or cyberbullying. If you are not sure what type of threat you may have on your device, run different types of security scans. Look up what the threat or threats mean. Remember to check all your devices for viruses.

This page will highlight common mistakes that you should not make and how to recognise the warning signs and make safer choices online.

This section will cover the main threats you are likely to encounter in a cyber-attack incident. It contains the different threat terms and the actions you should take.

Threat-term

Term-description

Do's

Adware

Adware is any software package that shows adverts, such as pop-ups. It generates revenue for its author and are harmful when clicked.

Do close adverts you don't recognise. Keep your device and apps updated. If lots of pop-ups suddenly appear, tell a parent, teacher or trusted adult.

Ransomware

Ransomware restricts user access to their files or device system by encrypting them or locking them down. It keeps this captive and demands a ransom to release it.

Do back up important files and keep your device updated. If your files become locked, tell a trusted adult or IT support.

Spyware

Spyware is usually hidden from a user and is used to collect data from a user's device, including information like websites visited, user logins and personal information.

Do check app permissions and only install trusted apps. Tell a trusted adult if you think someone is secretly watching you.

Trojan

A Trojan is disguised as something which the user wants or needs. It gives control of a user's device to a cyber criminal.

Do only download apps and files from trusted sources. Keep your security software turned on and updated.

Phishing

Phishing is when a fake entity impersonates one that a user trusts. It is used by cyber criminals to trick users into revealing information.

Do check messages before clicking links. Never give away passwords or personal information without checking first.

R.A.T

(Remote Access Trojan)

RATs are installed without the user knowing and are hidden to avoid detection. They allow a hacker to control a user's device remotely, and access the stored files or upload a virus.

Do only install trusted software. If someone seems to control your device, disconnect it from the internet and tell an adult.

Key-logger

Keyloggers collect a user's keyboard activity using algorithms that monitor the keys they type. This occurs on websites/applications that a user visits, or every key typed on their device.

Do keep your device protected and avoid suspicious websites. If you think your password was stolen, tell an adult and change it.
