Think SMART before you tap, type or click.

Dont

There are many things you dont do online. This page will inform you of all the things that you should'nt do online.

Some online actions can seem harmless at the time. But these can attract cyber criminals which leads to loss of your personal information, scams, account theft or cyberbullying. If you are not sure what type of threat you may have on your device, run different types of security scans. look up what the threat or threats mean. Remember to check all your devices for viruses.

This page will highlight common mistakes that you should not make and how to recognise the warning signs and make safer choices online.

This section will cover the main threats you are likely to encounter in a cyber-attack incident. It contains the different threat terms and the actions you shouldn't take.

Threat-term

Term-description

Don'ts

Adware

Adware is any software package that shows adverts, such as pop-ups. It generates revenue for its author and are harmful when clicked.

Don't click adverts you don't recognise or download anything they offer. Don't ignore repeated pop-ups or strange adverts.

Ransomware

Ransomware restricts user access to their files or device system by encrypting them or locking them down. it keeps this captive and demands a ransom to release it.

Don't pay a ransom without speaking to a trusted adult or IT support. Don't ignore warnings about locked or encrypted files.

Spyware

Spyware is usually hidden from a user and is used to collect data from a users device, including their information like websites visited, user logins and personal info.

Don't give apps unnecessary permissions or install apps from unknown sources. Don't ignore signs that your information is being watched.

Trojan

Trojan is disguised as something which the user wanted or needed. It gives control of a users device to a cyber-criminal.

Don't download unknown files, games or apps. Don't open attachments or downloads if you don't know where they came from.

Phishing

Phishing is when a fake entity inpersonates one that a user trusts. And is used by Cyber-criminals to trick users to reveal information.

Don't click suspicious links or reply to strange messages. Don't share passwords, bank details or personal information.

R.A.T

Remote Access Trojan

RATs are installed without the user knowing and are hidden to avoid detection. They allow a hacker to control a users device remotely, and access the stored files or upload a virus.

Don't allow strangers to control your device or install unknown software. Don't ignore unusual activity on your device.

Key-logger

Key-loggers collect users keyboard use with algorithms that monitor the keys they type. This occurs on websites/applications that a user visits, or every key typed on their device.

Don't type passwords or personal information into suspicious websites. Don't download unknown software that could record what you type.
